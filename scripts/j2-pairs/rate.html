      Annotation protocol v2, in short
      

        If I were the hiring manager for this job, how strongly would this CV
          make me want to interview the candidate?
        Judge fit for this job, from what is written.
      

      
    
        
  • 
          5 Strong fit: same role family, most must-haves incl. the main
          technology.
        
    • 
        
  • 
          4 Right role and main stack, but a notable must-have missing.
        
    • 
        
  • 
          3 Partial: related role or overlapping stack, about half the
          must-haves.
        
    • 
        
  • 2 A few shared skills, but a different job.
    • 
        
  • 1 No fit: different role family, only generic overlap.
    • 
      

      
    
        
  1. Rate alone; don't look at any model score.
    2. 
        
  2. Read the whole job first and note its must-haves, then the CV.
    3. 
        
  3. 
          About a minute per pair; unsure after two, pick the closest and add a
          note.
        
    4. 
        
  4. 
          No outside lookup. Ignore gender, age, nationality, writing style.
        
    5. 
      

      
    
        
  1. 
          Seniority moves a rating by one point at most. No experience for a 3+
          years role: at most 3.
        
    2. 
        
  2. Overqualified is not a penalty.
    3. 
        
  3. 
          A must-have that isn't written is missing, unless a named tool implies
          it (Django → Python).
        
    4. 
        
  4. 
          A CV that names no technologies is at most 2 for a technical job.
        
    5. 
        
  5. 
          Layout, length and English don't lower a rating (unless the job asks
          for an English level).
